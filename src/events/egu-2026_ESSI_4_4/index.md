## Overview

- [**Pangeo Session - ESSI4.4** https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/session/56059](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/session/56059)
- **When**: May 3–8, 2026
- **Format**: PICO (combines brief 2-minute oral pitches with interactive discussion at touch screens)
- **Abstract submission deadline**: January 15, 2026 at 13:00 CET

This session explores how Pangeo's open-source ecosystem and Discrete Global Grid Systems (DGGS) enable FAIR, scalable, and reusable workflows across earth and environmental sciences.

The PICO format is particularly effective for demonstrating workflows, demonstrations, and computational notebooks.

For more details, see the [announcement on the Pangeo Discourse](https://discourse.pangeo.io/t/call-for-abstracts-pangeo-dggs-session-at-egu26-pico-format/5468).

To submit an abstract, visit the [Session ESSI4.4 page](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/session/56059) on the EGU26 website and click "Submit your abstract".
